# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

Assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and provide evidence when disagreeing. Keep discussion relevant to the project and respect people's time and boundaries.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project publicly.

The current maintainer is [@MohammadWasi](https://github.com/MohammadWasi). A dedicated private conduct reporting channel has not yet been established. To request one, open a minimal [issue](https://github.com/MohammadWasi/proofRelay/issues) asking the maintainer to arrange private contact; omit names, incident details, and other personal information. GitHub's own reporting tools can be used for platform abuse. Do not use public issues to disclose another person's private information.

Maintainers may remove abusive content, restrict participation, or ban repeat offenders. Responses should be proportionate to the severity and pattern of behavior: clarification or a warning when appropriate, temporary restrictions for repeated violations, and removal for serious or persistent abuse. The maintainer should explain the reason privately where possible and limit disclosure of reports to what is necessary for handling them.

People affected by an enforcement decision may request reconsideration through the same reporting process. The project currently has a single maintainer and does not claim an independent appeal panel. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter or participant is unacceptable.
