# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

Assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and provide evidence when disagreeing. Keep discussion relevant to the project and respect people's time and boundaries.

Maintainers may remove abusive content, restrict participation, or ban repeat offenders. Enforcement should be proportionate and explain the reason privately where possible. Contact maintainers privately through the repository host for conduct concerns; do not include private information in public issues. No dedicated reporting address has been established in this initial source release.
